A lab that
never stops
experimenting

We are SporeLab — a small, chaotic, brilliant team from Catalunya. We build at the intersection of hard science, software and playful engineering.

The name is intentional. Spores don't wait for permission to grow. They drift, land somewhere unexpected, and flourish in surprising conditions. That's how we work.

Everything we build is open to explore. Some things are useful. Some are just beautiful. All of them started with a question we couldn't stop asking.
