// lab.projects.04 · Android

A family
meals organizer

"What are we having for dinner?" — every day, twice a day. Plan the whole week's lunches and dinners in one screen, with a separate line for each person, because kids don't always eat what the adults do. Your past meals become your best source of ideas.

◈ Coming Soon

01 · Plan

The whole week in one screen

Lunch and dinner side by side, one line per person. Mark who eats out, keep track of leftovers waiting in the fridge and add notes like "grandparents coming for lunch".

02 · Type

It remembers every dish

Start typing and the app suggests dishes from your own history. A couple of letters and the slot is filled.

03 · Recall

Your history, the best idea list

See how many times you've had each dish, when was the last time and for whom. Filter by family member when you need inspiration.

04 · Fill

Let AI fill the empty meals

Out of ideas? Claude or ChatGPT fills the empty slots based on what you usually eat, following your instructions: "fish twice, light dinners", "no fried food this week".

05 · Keep

Your menus stay on your phone

No account, no server. Back everything up to a single file and keep it wherever you like. The AI assistant is optional and uses your own API key.
